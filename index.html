Frequently Asked Questions

Are you set-aside eligible?

Yes. SBA-certified SDVOSB and VOSB: eligible for SDVOSB and VOSB set-asides, Veterans First sole-source, and total small business set-asides. CAGE 20R31 · UEI ELWUD2BV11M3 · SAM active.

Can you quote on a solicitation my company already holds?

Yes. We quote AV, audio, broadcast, IT and display line items for primes and other small businesses as a supplier or subcontractor, under a written teaming or supplier agreement with the solicitation, end customer and ship-to disclosed. See Primes & Teaming.

Do you install?

No. We are an equipment supplier. When a requirement calls for installation we arrange it through certified install partners as a separate line. We do not perform design-build.

How do you document TAA and Buy American compliance?

Per SKU, from the manufacturer's country-of-origin marking, on every quote. We never certify from a brand's nationality or a distributor's verbal answer, and we flag any line that cannot be made compliant before award.

Do you accept Government Purchase Cards?

Yes. Visa and Mastercard Government Purchase Cards for micro-purchases up to $15,000, no solicitation required.
